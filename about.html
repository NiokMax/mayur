About Mayur Bikash Gogoi

A little about who I am, my philosophy, and my multidisciplinary journey.

I'm Mayur Bikash Gogoi, a 20-year-old Computer Science and Engineering student at Nalbari Polytechnic, Assam, India.

I'm a developer, freelancer, copywriter, AI prompt engineer, and social media manager with over four years of experience in copywriting and web development. I enjoy both traditional coding and AI-assisted development, using the tools that best fit the project.

I'm interested in creating websites, developing software, exploring artificial intelligence, managing digital communities, and building online brands.

I'm also the founder of Loopni, a clothing and accessories brand that I'm developing alongside my studies and other projects.

My goal is to keep learning, improve my technical and creative abilities, and build meaningful digital products.

My Approach & Philosophy

Whether writing clean code or crafting concise copy, I believe in simplicity, clarity, and genuine usability. I don't believe in unnecessary complexity or superficial effects. Good software speaks through its responsiveness, speed, and real-world utility.

With modern AI tooling, development workflows are expanding rapidly. I embrace this by combining solid coding fundamentals (C, C++, Java, Python, JavaScript) with AI prompt engineering and modern automation workflows to build faster, smarter, and more effectively.
